Verify a passport

Enter the NSP ID printed on the card or certificate, or the card / certificate serial. Scanning the QR code opens this page with the signed token, which additionally proves the card was produced by this registry.

Every check is logged. Verification shows only the holder's name, photo, nationality, skills and passport status — never identity numbers or contact details.
